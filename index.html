You have a game in progress.

Reaching 0 lives is safe. The first time a player would go below zero, they survive on their doggy life instead. Next time, they're out.
By default, a killer hitting their own number does nothing. In hard mode it costs them lives too (single −1, double −2, triple −3).

Everyone starts on 0 lives. Hitting your own number builds lives up (single = +1, double = +2, triple = +3) — reach 3 and you become a killer. From then on, opponents hitting your number brings your lives back down the same way.
